## ADR-0011: Owner console scope, navigation and account governance

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-10-28, D-21; accepted as a decision, not implementation evidence). Earlier text: Proposed (owner decisions 2026-10-27; direction change the same day: online-store platform, startup phase with relaxed obligations; not implementation evidence)
- **Date:** 2026-10-27
- **Author:** Architecture + platform operations planning
- **Deciders:** Paduka Ongki (product owner), with security and privacy owners
- **Owner:** Bukalaris security owner with the product owner
- **Related:** PR-1, PR-15, PR-30; IAM-1, IAM-5, IAM-8, IAM-10, IAM-13, IAM-14, IAM-15; PRIV-2, PRIV-8, PRIV-9; UX-1, UX-9, UX-17, UX-20, UX-22; DATA-14; ARCH-5 SA-14 through SA-39, SA-50 through SA-52, UL-01, UL-02, HTTP-18 through HTTP-25, JOB-11 through JOB-13, JOB-19; [ADR-0005](ADR-0005-database-tenant-isolation-rls.md), [ADR-0008](ADR-0008-platform-architecture-and-routing.md), [ADR-0009](ADR-0009-platform-owned-affiliate-programme.md), [ADR-0010](ADR-0010-global-buyer-identity.md), [ADR-0012](ADR-0012-platform-tax-workflows.md); code review `CRSEC-02`, `CRSEC-04`, `CRSEC-06`; T-51, T-59, T-60, T-62, T-63, T-64, T-68, T-69

## Context

Owner decisions (2026-10-27):

1. The platform operator surface, previously "Super Admin" and, in the UI, "Bos", is renamed **Owner console** and lives at `owner.bukalaris.com`. The interim host labels `berkah` and `bos` were never deployed.
2. **Everything about sellers, affiliates and user accounts is managed from the Owner console:** stores (lifecycle, optional verification, plan and billing state, shipping account, domains, risk), affiliate accounts (applications, approval, suspension, rate bounds, payouts) and user accounts of every kind (seller team members, affiliate accounts, buyer accounts: view, support actions, suspend, recovery, merge requests, privacy requests). The owner asked for the navigation menu and requirements to be researched and perfected.
3. Tax on commissions and related items belongs to the platform workflows of [ADR-0012](ADR-0012-platform-tax-workflows.md), which stay dormant in the startup phase (see item 4); the console only reserves its entry and capability here.
4. **Direction change (same day):** Bukalaris is an **online-store platform** (Shopify-like), not a marketplace. Each store is operated by its own seller. Licensing and permit alignment will be adjusted later, and for the **startup phase obligations are relaxed**: seller verification is optional, store activation is never gated on verification, and nothing is gated on tax. This ADR therefore does not frame the platform as an electronic-commerce marketplace operator. Duties that exist only if that classification is adopted are kept as a switched-off, later "Compliance center" (Phase 2) with explicit activation criteria.

Evidence read for this ADR:

- **Bukalaris today.** The shell (`apps/web/src/components/admin-shell.tsx`, `apps/web/src/app/admin/layout.tsx`) is a flat three-item strip (Toko, Ongkir, Log System) with the brand "Bos". `/admin` renders the store list (SA-02); detail and creation are SA-03; logs SA-05/SA-06; shipping margin SA-07. `platform_operator` (identity) and `operator_grant` (only `logs.read`, a CHECK-constrained list) exist; `audit_log` has actor, nullable store, action and free-text reason only. Code review `CRSEC-06` records that no operator capability beyond `logs.read` exists (T-60 proposes the fix) and `CRSEC-02` that a single cookie/audience serves all surfaces (T-51/T-54/T-58).
- **Tokophi Super Admin (live reference, read-only research).** Pages: dashboard (MRR/ARR, merchant float, store status distribution, risk queue, pending approvals, activity), stores with lifecycle tabs and a health filter, store detail (billing, users, ledger movements, payouts, suspend with a required reason, comp, manual invoice), plan/billing with an invoice table, payout and refund queue, support tickets, analytics with cron liveness, settings (payment channels, couriers, PPN toggle, operators), audit log, update log. Lessons recorded there and adopted here: (a) the operator's authority is re-read from the database on every request, never taken from the cookie (a demoted or deleted operator kept minting sessions until this was fixed); (b) a route gate is the first statement of every page, above its first data read, because a summary of privileged data is itself privileged; an unlisted route fails closed and a test derives the route list from the directory; (c) every mutating action passes one capability gate and fails closed when the audit record cannot be written; (d) a suspension needs a reason, is a separate fact from billing and survives payment; (e) marking an invoice paid by hand is bookkeeping and does not grant entitlement (already PR-18); (f) payout completion is a compare-and-swap on the expected state, one statement decides and writes; (g) never mint, show or reset a password for someone else, and never report a credential that was not written; (h) impersonation was eventually reduced to a one-time exchange with a grant table, a 30-minute bound, dual-identity audit and fail-closed attribution, which is a large control surface that Bukalaris IAM-10 already declines to build unless controls exist; (i) the dashboard must not leak what the route floor withholds, and slices of a distribution must sum to the whole.

## Decision

### 1. Name and the naming collision

- The surface is the **Owner console** (UI brand "Owner", header `Bukalaris · Owner`). It lives at `owner.bukalaris.com` and keeps the internal route prefix `/admin` (ADR-0008). Glossary: *Owner console, formerly Super Admin / Bos; the interim host label `berkah` was never deployed; host `owner.bukalaris.com`*.
- The store-level membership role `owner` (`membership.role`, shown in Bahasa as "Pemilik toko", `IAM-2` "Seller Owner") is a **different thing**. Disambiguation rule for docs, code, tests and UI copy:
  - Platform side: **Owner console**, **platform owner** (the business owner or an Owner console staff member), capabilities named in `operator_grant`.
  - Store side: **store owner** / **Pemilik toko** / `membership.role = 'owner'`.
  - A bare "owner" is not allowed in new text without one of those qualifiers. Platform capabilities are never evaluated against `membership.role`, and a store owner never holds a platform capability because of the word.
- Existing identifiers are **not** renamed: ARCH-5 `SA-nn`, the `/admin` routes, `platform_operator`, `operator_grant`, `OperatorScope`, "Super Admin" in implemented rows and historical text. T-51/T-62 change user-visible copy only (header brand, `<title>`, nav label). The documentation terminology sweep of 2026-10-27 renamed the surface in current specification text and left historical records (tracks, build log, reviews, Done-task history) unchanged.
- `berkah` and `bos` are interim names, never deployed, kept reserved so nobody can claim them (ADR-0008). `owner` is the active Owner console host label and is also reserved, so no store slug or affiliate handle can claim it.

### 2. Scope: what the Owner console manages

The console is the only surface that reads or acts across tenants, affiliates and buyers. It is **not** a seller, affiliate or buyer view and never renders their surfaces. Management domains, each with a capability family (IAM-15) and screens (UX-22, ARCH-5):

| Domain | Managed here | Authoritative records stay in |
|---|---|---|
| Stores | Create, approve, suspend, reactivate, verification record (optional), plan and billing state, comp, Bukalaris-generated Partner Mengantar seller account status, domains (later), risk flags, notes/cases | `store`, DATA-10, DATA-9 |
| Affiliates | Applications, approve/reject/suspend/reactivate, rate bounds and `affiliate_hold_days` (decided default 3 days), attribution disputes and fraud flags, programme payouts | DATA-11 (ADR-0009) |
| User accounts | Search, masked view, status, support actions, identifier conflict review (staff-executed merge and ownership recovery decided disabled in Phase 1, D6), privacy requests | Auth tables, `membership`, DATA-11, DATA-12 (ADR-0010) |
| Money | Seller ledger float and payouts, buyer refunds, affiliate payouts, subscription invoices, shipping margin, shipping discounts and cashback split rules, Mengantar Partner account sync/health, COD reconciliation batches (SA-53) and payment reconciliation | DATA-7, DATA-9, DATA-10, DATA-11 |
| Tax center | Reserved menu group gated by `tax.*` capabilities; in the startup phase only the subset UX-23 marks startup (overview and settings, earnings statements, voluntary identity list) is routable, everything else is dormant until activation; screens are owned by UX-23 | DATA-15 (ADR-0012) |
| Compliance center (Phase 2) | Verification queue, takedown notices, complaints, privacy requests, incident register, registrations/retention | DATA-14 |
| Platform and operations | Reserved labels, host config, provider health, rate limits, logs, audit viewer, job/outbox/webhook health, backup status, **issue reports** ("Lapor masalah" control in every page header, queue and per-page function health), optional public **Update Log** authoring (Phase 1.5) | existing and DATA-14 |
| Staff | Owner console staff, capability grants, access reviews, approvals | `platform_operator`, `operator_grant` (T-60), DATA-14 |

The console writes to another context only through that context's narrow service (T-59 platform grants). It does not read another context's tables directly in page code, and it does not move authoritative state into DATA-14: DATA-14 holds only history, cases, approvals, verification evidence and access records.

### 3. Navigation and the Phase 1 / Phase 2 split

Navigation is capability-gated groups (full tree with routes, ids, capabilities and states in UX-22):

**Overview** (Dashboard, Approvals) · **Stores** · **Affiliates** · **Accounts** · **Money** · **Tax center** (slot reserved) · **Content and compliance** · **Platform** · **Operations** · **Staff and access**.

- **Phase 1 (startup core, tasks T-62 and T-63, with the issue-report queue in T-68):** dashboard, approvals inbox, stores (list, create, detail, optional verification record, risk queue, ledger view), cases and notes, accounts (search, masked detail, support actions, suspend, recovery link, session revoke, MFA reset request), money screens as their owning tasks land, affiliate screens delivered by T-54, platform settings, operations (logs, audit viewer, health, issue reports), the persistent "Lapor masalah" header control, staff and grants.
- **Phase 1.5 (optional, T-69):** Update Log authoring in the console, the public read-only site at `logs.bukalaris.com` (not the internal Log System) and the "Apa yang baru" indicator. Host dispatch is owned by ADR-0008.
- **Phase 2 (T-64, switched off until its activation criteria hold):** verification queue, takedown notices, complaints, privacy-request queue, incident register with the notification clock, registrations/retention board, domain management, public report intake, privacy/audit export downloads, affiliate dispute and offer directories.
- **Tax center:** `/admin/tax` is reserved in the menu and gated by `tax.*` capabilities; only the startup subset defined by UX-23 and ADR-0012 is routable in startup mode and the rest returns not-found until the owner activates tax workflows. No tax screen is specified here.
- A Phase 2 item is **absent from the menu and its route returns not-found** until activated by an audited platform setting that records who, when and which criterion was met. It is never shown as a disabled teaser that suggests an obligation.

**Compliance center activation criteria (T-64):** all of (a) an owner or advisor decision recorded in `DECISIONS.md` that the compliance workflows apply, either because the platform is classified as an electronic-system or marketplace operator after licensing alignment (UNVERIFIED, advisor decision) or because the owner opts in voluntarily; (b) PSE/TDPSE and related registration status recorded by the owner (OPEN, never invented); (c) intake channel, response-time policy and retention policy approved by the privacy owner (numbers OPEN); (d) a named incident coordinator and privacy contact (PRIV-5); (e) T-63 delivered, so cases, status history and the audit extension exist. Until then complaints, notices and privacy requests are handled as ordinary **cases** (Phase 1) with a due date field.

### 4. Account governance

- **Roles are presets, capabilities are authority.** IAM-15 defines Owner console staff roles as named bundles of capabilities; the server checks capabilities from `operator_grant` on every request, re-read from the database, never from the cookie or the role name (T-60).
- **Least privilege and separation.** Read, act, approve and grant are separate capabilities. Nobody approves their own request. Capabilities touching money, tax, personal-data export and staff access need a second human (four-eyes) as set in IAM-15; in a one-person team those actions stay pending rather than self-approve (small-team fallback is OPEN, not adopted).
- **Every action is audited with a reason**, in the same transaction as the mutation, and fails closed if the audit row cannot be written (SEC-11). The audit row names actor, capability used, target kind and id, case reference, reason code and outcome.
- **Support access without impersonation.** There is no "sign in as", no password or recovery-code reveal, no session transfer, no cookie minted on `app`, `affiliate` or `buyer` hosts, and no reset-then-login flow. Support uses (a) a **masked, read-only diagnostic view** inside the Owner console, tied to a case and a purpose (Phase 1), and (b) a **consented, time-boxed support grant** that the seller owner creates in Seller Admin to let named staff see specific screens read-only inside the Owner console (OPEN: not adopted until the product owner approves consent UX, duration and screen list). Recovery uses a link sent to the verified contact on file; the operator never sees it. **Prerequisite:** support-issued recovery links go through `mail_outbox`, which today stores URLs in clear text readable by the app role (`CRSEC-04`); the recovery-link action stays disabled until the fix lands (real mailer, bodies purged after delivery, no app-role `SELECT`; T-73); T-63 depends on it. Staff identities are not support targets: an identity with a `platform_operator` row is excluded from every `accounts.*` capability and is handled only through `staff.manage` with four-eyes (IAM-15).
- **Privacy.** Personal data is masked by default in the data layer; revealing a field needs a purpose and a case, is time-boxed and audited (PRIV-9). Break-glass is an approval-gated, expiring elevation that alerts the audit capability holders (IAM-15, PRIV-9).
- **MFA and session isolation.** Owner console sessions require MFA, use a host-only `__Host-` cookie and a distinct audience on `owner.bukalaris.com`, and re-authenticate for sensitive actions. No seller, affiliate or buyer session grants any Owner capability, and Owner capabilities grant no seller, affiliate or buyer power (IAM-8, IAM-13, IAM-14, `CRSEC-02`). Bootstrap and recovery of the first staff are out-of-band, by two named people (IAM-15, RUNBOOK).
- **Suspension semantics** are kept distinct and never conflated: store operator suspension (`store.suspended_at`, PR-18, independent of billing), affiliate suspension (unpublishes the affiliate storefront, deactivates its links and codes, stops new attribution, never deletes earned money, DATA-11, IAM-13), seller team-member access removal (membership), buyer account restriction (ADR-0010, never silent merge), and platform-wide account suspension of an auth identity (all sessions revoked in the same transaction).

### 5. Startup relaxations (explicit, reversible)

- Store verification is an **optional record**: a store can be created, approved, activated and paid out without it. Any later gate (for example payout release requiring a verified record) is a product decision recorded separately and is OPEN.
- Nothing is gated on tax. Tax identity before the first affiliate payout is no longer a startup rule; it is the policy flag `tax_identity_required_for_payout`, default OFF (BILL-8, ADR-0012, DATA-15). Stores have no tax-identity gate.
- Compliance center screens are Phase 2 as above.
- Security, audit, masking, MFA and four-eyes for money are **not relaxed**: they are not marketplace-specific. Four-eyes sits on the decision that moves money ("approve for transfer"), not on recording it afterwards (IAM-15).
- **Minimal non-KYC payout fraud controls stay mandatory (decided, D5).** *Plain terms:* Bukalaris manages 100% of order payments and shipping under one unified system (prepaid payments through our AutoLaris gateway and COD through Bukalaris-generated Mengantar Partner seller accounts with platform master account fallback; there is no seller-pasted external Mengantar key and no two-system split). Because Bukalaris holds all seller and affiliate balances before payout, a thief who takes over a login, changes the bank account to their own and requests a payout could drain the balance before the real owner notices. Decided controls for all seller and affiliate payouts: (a) **holder-name check**: the bank account holder name must match the account's registered holder or the payee's name before a payout (a mismatch holds the payout for review); (b) **72-hour hold on a new or changed bank destination** (`payout_destination`, `affiliate_payout_destination`, default 72 hours, configurable in the Owner console) before a payout can use it, and the same hold after any credential recovery or MFA reset; (c) **notify the old contact** when a bank destination or verified contact changes; (d) **step-up**: bank changes and payout requests need fresh proof (IAM-9). These are anti-fraud controls, not tax, licensing or identity verification, and do not depend on the optional verification record.
- **Staff-executed store-ownership recovery and buyer merge: decided disabled in Phase 1 (D6).** *Plain terms:* ownership recovery means a staff member changes who owns a store, and a merge means a staff member joins two buyer accounts so one person sees the other's order history; the abuse scenario is a staff member being socially engineered into handing a store, its payouts or a buyer's order history to the wrong person. Both staff-executed actions stay **disabled in Phase 1** (routes return not-found): locked-out sellers use **self-service recovery only** (reset via the registered email or phone OTP, MFA recovery codes), which never needs staff to change who owns a store; buyer accounts merge only via **self-service proof of both identifiers** (phone and email).
- **Issue-report screenshots: decided.** The owner delegated this decision; Phase 1 accepts **no screenshot or attachment** in the "Lapor masalah" report (text, the element picker and the automatically captured allowlisted context only). Attachments become a Phase 2 option only with a PII warning, the PRIV-9 purpose rules, byte and pixel caps, scrubbing, a finite retention and a dedicated capability (UX-22 "Screenshots and attachments").
- UU PDP duties for personal data the platform holds apply conceptually to the data the console touches; their statutory interpretation (breach notification, erasure, consent) is UNVERIFIED and an advisor item, not a startup blocker.

### 6. Data and architecture hand-offs

- DATA-14 defines the records (cases/notes, account status history, support actions, verification records, approval requests, personal-data access events, break-glass grants, platform audit extension; Phase 2: notices, complaints, privacy requests, incidents, registrations). All are `platform`-class tables under T-59: no seller or affiliate role grants, writes through named services.
- ARCH-5 SA-14 through SA-39 register the screens as Planned. SA-02 moves from `/admin` to `/admin/stores` when SA-14 takes `/admin` (T-62); the implemented rows are untouched until then.

## Alternatives considered

- *Keep the name Super Admin / Bos* — rejected by the owner decision; "Bos" is also an informal title that collides with store-level vocabulary.
- *Rename the store `owner` role to avoid the collision* — rejected: it is stored data (`membership_role_chk`), used by IAM-2/IAM-8 and tests; qualifiers solve the ambiguity at no migration cost.
- *Impersonation with a one-time exchange (Tokophi's final design)* — rejected: it needs grants, expiry, dual-identity audit and fail-closed attribution on a surface with no current controls; IAM-10 already says it stays disabled unless those controls exist. The masked diagnostic view serves the support job with no session power.
- *Role-only authorization (support / admin / owner ladder)* — rejected: code review `CRSEC-06` and T-60 already require per-capability grants; roles remain only as presets.
- *Build the Compliance center first* — rejected by the direction change: its duties are conditional on licensing alignment and would add startup gates.
- *One generic "settings" screen with feature flags* — rejected: no concrete flag exists; live-write gates (for example the Mengantar write switch) stay environment-controlled and are shown read-only.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** one practical, capability-gated back-office with a clear startup core; support without session power; consistent audit and masking; compliance features can be added without re-architecting.
- **Negative:** four-eyes needs at least two staff for money actions; many new `platform`-class tables; a rename touches copy, tests and a few documents; Phase 2 items need an activation switch and its own audit.
- **Open (OPEN, owner or advisor):** small-team fallback for four-eyes; consented support grant UX, duration and screens; numeric reveal window, fresh-authentication window and session lifetime; thresholds (if any) below which a single approver is acceptable for money actions; whether payout release will ever require verification; if a future phase ever enables staff-executed ownership recovery or buyer merge, its evidence standard (notice to the verified contact, cooling-off period, freeze on bank changes and payouts after recovery); licensing posture and registration status; response-time policy numbers; who is the incident coordinator and privacy contact. The COD reconciliation batch approval (`payouts.reconcile`, four-eyes, SA-53) and the shipping margin, discount and cashback split rules (SA-07) are specified in UX-17, UX-22 and IAM-15.
- **UNVERIFIED (secondary sources, advisor confirmation required):** that PP 80/2019 duties (merchant identity, complaint handling, notice and takedown, secure system, sanctions) apply to this platform's classification; UU PDP 27/2022 duties (breach notification within 3x24 hours to subjects and the authority, erasure and destruction, consent withdrawal) and their implementing details; PSE private-scope registration timing (Permenkominfo 5/2020 jo 10/2021, OSS RBA, TDPSE number shown on the site). None is asserted as a statutory requirement by this ADR.
