## ADR-0006: Platform-managed money, net seller proceeds ledger, and Mengantar shipping reconciliation

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-10-28, DECISIONS D-20; accepted as a decision, not implementation evidence: implementation status is in TASKS.md). Earlier text: Proposed (owner direction 2026-10-27: hybrid Mengantar shipping accounts, superseded the same day by the unified Bukalaris-managed system of D-15; platform subscription billing specified separately by PR-18 / BILL-7)

## Context

Bukalaris earns shipping margin through a platform-owned Mengantar aggregator account (Q-12, Q-2) and serves COD-first sellers nationwide (Q-13). COD cash collected by the courier is settled to Bukalaris under its Mengantar accounts (the settlement sweep mechanics of Partner-provisioned accounts are UNVERIFIED, OPEN-F9); prepaid payments use the platform's AutoLaris gateway. That custody requires an auditable, replay-safe accounting boundary before the first real COD shipment (Q-17, T-37). Under the owner decision of 2026-10-27 (DECISIONS D-15, [ADR-0007](ADR-0007-hybrid-mengantar-shipping-accounts.md)) there is one unified Bukalaris-managed shipping and money system: every store ships through a Bukalaris-provisioned Partner Mengantar account (`partner_managed`) with the platform master account (`platform_master`) as fallback, so prepaid and COD orders both run through this single ledger. There are no seller-owned shipping accounts; an earlier hybrid design that had them is superseded (see Alternatives considered).

Reviewing the production-proven finance architecture in the Etalashop reference pack (`FINANCE.md`, `0004-platform-managed-payments-shipping.md`, `0015-net-seller-proceeds-and-shipping-reconciliation.md`) and the Tokophi repository rules it was extracted from (`AGENTS.md` "Order lifecycle" and "Shipping & COD", `specs/docs/KIRIMINAJA.md`, `specs/docs/FLOWS.md`, the 2026 audits) exposed ten failure modes that must be prevented by design:

1. Crediting gross `payments.total` instead of merchandise proceeds leaks gateway fees into seller withdrawable balance.
2. Releasing funds at `shipped` instead of `completed` (delivered), or releasing COD proceeds before the carrier cash is reconciled, allows sellers to withdraw funds for parcels that later return (`returned` / RTS) or for cash that never arrived.
3. Mutating balance rows in place or running concurrent withdrawal requests without a store-level transactional lock allows double-credit on retried callbacks and double-withdrawal under race conditions.
4. Showing raw third-party provider brand names (`Mengantar`, `AutoLaris`) outside the single one-time onboarding disclosure allowed by decision 1 leaks platform vendor structure.
5. Treating AutoLaris `advice` responses as direct settlement mutations can falsely fail or settle active bills until vendor status codes are confirmed in production.
6. Reversing a `pending` sale against the `available` balance (or the reverse) drives withdrawable balance negative while the held pool keeps the money (Tokophi `FLOWS.md`).
7. A status writer that silently rejects a courier-driven multi-step jump (for example `shipped -> completed` seen in one poll) freezes a delivered COD order uncredited while the polling job reports success; a permissive writer allows `completed -> cancelled`, which restores stock and claws back cash already in hand (Tokophi `AUDIT_ORDER_STOCK_DISPATCH.md`, `AGENTS.md`).
8. Late or replayed provider callbacks: a late `paid` on a cancelled order takes money with no credit and no refund path; a late `expired` regressing a `paid` payment un-collects money (Tokophi `BUILD-LOG.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`).
9. A balance sum that joins `order` silently drops `payout` entries (their `order_id` is NULL by design); Tokophi measured the available balance jumping from Rp21.0M to Rp43.7M.
10. Ambiguous vendor status semantics: Tokophi's vendor documentation disagreed on whether status `200` means picked up or delivered, and mapping it to delivered collects COD cash and credits the ledger for a parcel still in transit (Tokophi `BACKLOG.md`).

## Decision

1. **Platform-managed providers and white-label brand boundary.**
   - AutoLaris prepaid payments and Mengantar shipping are **100% platform-managed by Bukalaris** (one unified system; no `own_key` external seller keys and no System A / System B split). Under ADR-0007, Bukalaris provisions a Partner-managed Mengantar seller account (`partner_managed`, `POST /api/partner/v1/sellers`) for every store — even sellers who previously had an external Mengantar account must use the new Bukalaris-generated account — with the platform master Mengantar account (`platform_master`) as fallback, so 100% of shipping management, courier discounts, margin, and shipping cashback are controlled by Bukalaris in the Owner console (`SA-07`).
   - Merchant-facing and buyer-facing UI, emails, and order status pages display platform labels (`Bukalaris Kirim` and `Bukalaris Bayar`), enforced by an automated source/copy test. If Mengantar's Partner API agreement requires naming Mengantar in the store onboarding terms / confirmation email for the provisioned partner account, that one-time onboarding disclosure is the only allowed mention (ADR-0007 decision 6). Every other merchant/buyer surface stays white-label. The Owner console (`/admin`) retains vendor names and references for operations and reconciliation.

2. **Immutable checkout financial allocation (`order_financials`).**
   - Every order freezes an immutable `order_financials` row in the same transaction as `order` creation; the row is never updated afterwards. The provisioned shipping account is stored **once, as a foreign key**: `order.shipping_account_id` (compound `(store_id, shipping_account_id)` to `store_shipping_account`, DATA-9) plus an immutable `shipping_account_kind` snapshot (`partner_managed` or `platform_master`) on both `order` and `order_financials`; booking never silently switches accounts.
   - Seller-withdrawable proceeds are goods proceeds plus the goods sales tax collected for the seller (tax already inside prices **and** tax added on top; ADR-0012 section 6 leaves both with the seller), less any affiliate commission, plus any seller share of shipping cashback (`seller_cashback_idr`) configured by the Owner console (`ADR-0007`, `BILL-6`). Shipping fees charged to the buyer, platform shipping margin, platform shipping cashback (`platform_cashback_idr`), COD handling fees, COD-fee VAT, and payment gateway admin fees are platform-controlled money. All amounts are integer IDR; the per-line allocation of discounts and included tax and the rounding rule of each component are defined once in BILL-4:

```text
-- frozen once in order_financials (checkout transaction)
goods_proceeds              = subtotal - goods_discount - included_sales_tax
shipping_charged            = checkout_shipping - shipping_discount
payable                     = goods_proceeds + included_sales_tax + added_sales_tax
                              + shipping_charged + cod_fee + cod_fee_vat
                              (= order.total; the gateway admin fee is separate:
                               buyer_payable = payable + admin_fee)
sale_amount                 = goods_proceeds + included_sales_tax + added_sales_tax
                              - affiliate_commission
shipping_cashback_gross     = verified partner/courier shipping cashback for the order (default 0)
seller_cashback             = seller share from Owner console cashback_rule (default 0)
platform_cashback           = shipping_cashback_gross - seller_cashback

-- recorded once at booking in the separate shipment_cost record
shipping_delta              = booked_shipping - shipping_charged
seller_shipping_debit       = max(0, shipping_delta)
platform_shipping_margin    = max(0, -shipping_delta)

-- derived, never stored
seller_entitlement          = sale_amount + seller_cashback
                              + SUM(shipping_debit entries)
                              + SUM(shipping_debit_reversal entries)
                              (debit entries are negative, their reversals positive)
```

   - **Allocation identity** (DATA-7 CHECK at checkout): `payable = sale_amount + affiliate_commission + shipping_charged + cod_fee + cod_fee_vat`, i.e. every rupiah the buyer pays (excluding the gateway admin fee) is either seller money, an affiliate obligation, or platform money. At booking: `shipping_charged + seller_shipping_debit = booked_shipping + platform_shipping_margin`.
   - The `sale` ledger entry amount is `sale_amount` read from the frozen `order_financials` row; it never depends on booking. Any `seller_cashback` is booked as a separate `cashback` ledger entry (`ledger_one_cashback_per_order`), and the shipping debit is its own ledger entry, so shipping is never deducted twice and the entitlement is derived from `sale` + `cashback` + shipping entries instead of being stored. The booked cost lives in the append-once `shipment_cost` record (DATA-7, one per order), which keeps `order_financials` immutable.
   - **One unified Bukalaris money system (ADR-0007, BILL-6):** All stores and orders (both prepaid via our AutoLaris gateway and COD via Bukalaris-provisioned Partner Mengantar accounts or the platform master account) run through this single Bukalaris ledger. There is no `own_key` external seller account and no "System A vs System B" split.

   - Store-funded `goods_discount` and `shipping_discount` are persisted separately so voucher/free-shipping promotions never corrupt the allocation math.
   - A buyer-facing COD handling fee is **optional and OFF by default** (`cod_fee_idr = 0`). Observed Mengantar account estimates already include the provider's COD fee in the courier cost (`codFee` 4 994 at `COD_AMOUNT` 150 000, identical across couriers at that amount; `docs/tracks/shipping.md`), and the T-34 margin rule folds that cost into the buyer shipping fee. Enabling a second buyer fee would double-charge the COD cost unless the platform deliberately re-prices.
   - When platform policy enables it, the base is `B = (subtotal - goods_discount) + shipping_charged`: goods after store-funded discount plus shipping payable after discount. **Goods sales tax (`added_sales_tax`) is outside the base — no tax-on-tax** (Etalashop `FINANCE.md` §3, which supersedes the tax-inclusive base and the `x 0.03 / 0.97` gross-up recorded in Etalashop ADR 0015):
     - `cod_fee_idr = max(min_cod_fee_idr, round_half_up(B * cod_fee_rate_bps / 10000))`; the rate is an integer in basis points (platform policy `cod_fee_rate_bps`), never a fractional rate. The floor exists because a straight rate under-recovers the provider's minimum on small baskets (Etalashop lost Rp1 110 per Rp50 000 parcel before adding it), and the vendor rate differs per courier, so one rate is honest only for the couriers actually offered for COD.
     - `cod_fee_vat_idr` comes from the effective, confirmed `vat_output` `tax_policy_version` for the revenue line `cod_fee` (DATA-15, BILL-9 W7/W8), using that policy's rate and `rounding` field. It is `0`, with no policy reference, whenever `ppn_enabled` is false, no effective version exists, or (when `pkp_gate_enabled` is ON) the platform profile is not a confirmed PKP. **No VAT rate appears in this ADR, in code or in seeds.** The applied rate and policy version are snapshotted in `order_financials` (`cod_fee_vat_rate_bps`, `cod_fee_vat_policy_version_id`).
     - These optional fee allocations stay in platform money (`order_financials`) inside the buyer grand total. Mengantar documents the Create Order collection formula as **COD value = goods value + shipping fee + COD fee**; this is distinct from an unverified fee rate, floor or VAT treatment. Read the account's dynamic `codFee` from `/order/estimate`; the Etalashop rate/VAT example above is not a verified Mengantar tariff (decision 10).

3. **Append-only seller ledger (`ledger_entry`) and derived balance.**
   - `ledger_entry` is an append-only book under `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` (`store_id`). No row is ever updated in place except a controlled status transition from `pending` to `available` on release; reversals always insert a new opposing signed entry.
   - Balances are **derived, never stored as a mutable counter**:
     - `available_balance_idr = SUM(amount_idr) WHERE status = 'available'`
     - `pending_balance_idr = SUM(amount_idr) WHERE status = 'pending'`
   - Entry kinds: `sale` (seller merchandise proceeds credit, amount `sale_amount`), `cashback` (seller's share of shipping cashback configured by Bukalaris in the Owner console, `seller_cashback_idr`, when > 0), `shipping_debit` (booking cost above buyer-charged shipping), `shipping_debit_reversal` (exact opposing credit when the provider confirms the booking cost was voided or refunded), `return_reversal` (claws back a still-`pending` prepaid `sale` when the order is `cancelled` or `returned` in transit; it never reverses an `available` sale), `payout` (negative debit when seller requests withdrawal), `payout_reversal` (exact opposing credit when the Owner console rejects a payout), and `adjustment` (audited operator correction; always four-eyes with an approved `approval_request_id` and a unique business reference).
   - Database partial unique indexes enforce idempotency:
     - `ledger_one_sale_per_order` on `(store_id, order_id) WHERE kind = 'sale'`
     - `ledger_one_cashback_per_order` on `(store_id, order_id) WHERE kind = 'cashback'`
     - `ledger_one_return_per_order` on `(store_id, order_id) WHERE kind = 'return_reversal'`
     - `ledger_one_shipping_debit_per_order` on `(store_id, order_id) WHERE kind = 'shipping_debit'` and `ledger_one_shipping_reversal_per_order` on `(store_id, order_id) WHERE kind = 'shipping_debit_reversal'` (one booking per order; rebooking is not supported, see decision 7)
     - `ledger_one_payout_per_payout` on `(store_id, payout_id) WHERE kind = 'payout'` and `ledger_one_payout_reversal_per_payout` on `(store_id, payout_id) WHERE kind = 'payout_reversal'`: a replayed rejection finds its existing reversal and inserts nothing
     - `ledger_adjustment_business_ref` unique on `(store_id, business_ref) WHERE kind = 'adjustment'` (immutable business reference)
     - `payout_request_key` unique on `payout (store_id, request_key)`: a replayed payout request returns the first payout instead of debiting twice
   - Every writer uses `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` against these indexes; a double credit is a database impossibility, not a code convention.
   - **Ledger consistency invariants (contract, owner decision D-21; Acceptance, not existing code):** the single-entry append-only shape above is retained. Balances are always derived by `SUM(amount_idr)` over `ledger_entry`, never a stored counter. A read-only verification command, `pnpm verify:ledger` (to be built by T-37; decision 9), and an automated test must recompute per store and prove: exactly one `sale` per order that has met the booking condition of decision 4 (never zero for such an order, never two); at most one `return_reversal` per order; `available` never negative; every `payout` and `payout_reversal` entry pairs with a `payout` row; every released entry has `released_at` and `release_basis`; and no query path joins `order` to compute a balance. The canonical wording is the DATA-7 Acceptance ([DATA-7](05-DATA-MODEL.md#data-7--financial-snapshot-append-only-ledger-payouts-and-refunds)); the BILL-5 Acceptance links to it.

4. **Booking, release, and return rules.**
   - **Unified custody scope:** All stores ship under Bukalaris-managed accounts (`partner_managed` generated via the Mengantar Partner API or `platform_master` fallback, ADR-0007; partner settlement/cashback sweep gate `OPEN-F9`). Every order's `sale`, `cashback`, and shipping-debit entries run through this single Bukalaris ledger.
   - **Prepaid orders:** `sale` entry (and `cashback` when > 0) is booked as `pending` when payment reaches `paid` (`payments.amount_idr` = order payable excluding gateway admin fee); `sale` releases to `available` when the order is delivered (`completed`), and `cashback` releases when delivered and shipping cashback is reconciled. Mengantar shipment booking is refused until payment is `paid`.
   - **COD orders:** no ledger credit is created at order placement or shipment dispatch, and nothing is ever credited for a parcel still in transit. COD is a fact derived from the order's payment method, never a request parameter: the booking service derives the cash-collection amount from the order and refuses a caller that disagrees. The COD payment is marked `paid` and the `sale` (and `cashback` when > 0) ledger entry is booked as `pending` **only when the provider's `DELIVERED` status** (webhook or poll) transitions the order to `completed` through the status writer. A seller or operator manual "complete" never books a `sale`: it records a completion claim that leaves the order short of `completed`. When the provider status is unavailable, an Owner console operator may request manual completion as a four-eyes `approval_request` (action `cod.manual_complete`, IAM-15 matrix) that carries a courier-proof evidence reference; only after a different approver approves it does the writer complete the order with `source = manual_approved`. A seller can never complete a COD order for credit.
   - **Release rule (owner decision 2026-10-27; supersedes the fixed H+1 release of D-11 and removes `cod_release_delay_hours`):** seller money becomes `available` only for parcels that are **delivered and reconciled**. A COD `sale` (and any `cashback` entry) booked `pending` at delivery is released to `available` only when the carrier cash/cashback for that order is reconciled (RECON). A prepaid `sale` (booked `pending` at `paid`) is released at delivered. Nothing releases at `shipped` or while in transit. The scheduled worker job `ledger.release` releases reconciled COD/cashback entries and delivered prepaid sales, idempotently, stamping `released_at` and `release_basis` (`reconciliation` or `delivery`): for each candidate it takes the row lock (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) and updates only `WHERE status = 'pending' AND NOT EXISTS (a return_reversal for the same store and order)` and the basis condition holds.
   - **Reconciliation signal (evidence-gated, UNVERIFIED, OPEN-F8):** Mengantar's order object carries `isReconciliated` and its invoice list has the type `typeReconciliation`; both are documented but their semantics are not, and the sandbox simulation never triggers reconciliation. Until the signal is observed in production or confirmed by Mengantar and the owner accepts it, the platform setting `provider_reconciliation_enabled` is `false` and the only release path is an **Owner console reconciliation batch**: a `reconciliation_batch` (DATA-7) records the provider evidence references (for example carrier invoice lines with amount and date), carries a four-eyes approval (action `cod.reconcile_release`, capability `payouts.reconcile`, IAM-15), and lists per-order `reconciliation_batch_item` rows with `reconciled_at` and `reconciliation_source = owner_batch`. When the provider signal is verified and enabled, the same item is written with `reconciliation_source = provider` by a system batch through the same writer; no other code path may mark a COD sale reconcilable. `cod.manual_complete` (manual delivery completion) is a separate four-eyes action and is never part of a batch.
   - **Return (`returned` / RTS) and cancellation:** adds an explicit `returned` order state reachable **only from in-transit states** (`shipped -> returned`); `completed -> returned` and `completed -> cancelled` are illegal. Entering `returned`, or `cancelled` before delivery, restores variant stock once and marks uncollected COD payment as `failed`. A **COD** parcel that returns in transit, is refused or is cancelled before delivery never earned a credit, so there is nothing to reverse and no negative balance. A **prepaid** order inserts one `return_reversal` equal to `-sale.amount_idr` against the still-`pending` sale (pending against pending, so both net to zero in the held pool) and opens the buyer refund (decision 5). Because release only happens at or after delivery and a return only before it, a reversal never meets an `available` sale and `available_balance_idr` can never go negative by construction. A booked shipping debit stays as recorded (decision 7 coverage rule, OPEN-F6).
   - **Return versus delivery race:** the status writer locks the order row first, so delivery (which books or releases the sale) and return (which reverses the pending sale) are serialized through it; the release job additionally locks the sale row and refuses a sale that already has a reversal. Neither writer acts on a status it did not lock.
   - **Lock order (every money writer, never reversed):** (1) the order row `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`; (2) the per-store payout lock `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('bukalaris.payout.store'), hashtext(store_id::text))`; (3) ledger rows `FOR UPDATE` in ascending id; (4) the per-affiliate lock `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('bukalaris.payout.affiliate'), hashtext(affiliate_id::text))` (ADR-0009 section 6). A writer only acquires locks later in this list than those it already holds. The two-key form gives each lock class its own namespace in the 32-bit key space, so a store id and an affiliate id can never contend for the same key. **No provider call is made while a transaction or lock is open** (decision 7).
   - **Post-delivery returns and refunds are out of ledger scope in M3.** A parcel that comes back after `completed` creates no automatic ledger event; if ever needed it is a four-eyes `adjustment` plus a manual buyer refund handled as a finance case. A late claim after a payout raises an explicit conflict that needs manual finance correction, never a silent reversal (99ribu precedent). Partial returns and partial refunds are unsupported.
   - **No negative balance by construction (owner decision, closes the former OPEN-F1):** see the release and return rules above; the only ways to reduce an `available` balance are a payout request and a four-eyes `adjustment`.
   - **COD policy (owner decision, closes the former OPEN-F2):** COD is offered only for courier services whose return is free in both directions. The rule is data-driven: the allow-list `courier_service_policy` (DATA-9) carries `cod_allowed` and `return_free` per courier service, currently set so that only SPX qualifies, and **no courier name is hard-coded**. Checkout and booking refuse COD for a service that is not `cod_allowed AND return_free`. Nobody bears return shipping, so no return-shipping debit exists in M3. The belief that SPX returns are free is **UNVERIFIED** (Tokophi flagged the same): it is a live-use gate (OPEN-F7). Confirm the SPX terms with Mengantar before the first real COD parcel; if it proves false, revisit this decision.

5. **Payouts and non-COD buyer refunds.**
   - Only a store `owner` (`settings.write` / `finance.payout`) can request a payout above `min_payout_idr`, and only to a payout destination (DATA-7 `payout_destination`) that is `active`, whose hold has elapsed, and after a step-up (fresh proof per IAM-9, see the destination controls below).
   - The request acquires the per-store payout lock in the decision-4 order, verifies `available_balance_idr >= amount_idr` (a payout can only draw on `available`, which is never negative), snapshots the destination onto the `payout` row, and inserts a negative `payout` ledger entry with that `payout_id` in the same transaction, guarded by the unique request key.
   - The Owner console processes payouts (`requested -> processing -> paid | rejected`). **`requested -> processing` is "approve for transfer" and is the four-eyes step**: an `approval_request` (action `payout.approve_transfer`, capability `payouts.process`, IAM-15) whose payload hash binds the payout id, the amount and the bank snapshot, which the approver sees; its id is stored on the payout row as `approval_request_id`. The manual bank transfer is made only after that approval. **Operator claim lease & non-reusable transfer evidence (99ribu precedent):** an operator executing a `processing` payout acquires an exclusive **30-minute claim lease** (`claimed_by_user_id`, `claim_expires_at = now() + 30 min`, concurrent claims rejected with 409 `PAYOUT_ALREADY_CLAIMED`) after a single-use step-up reauthentication proof (`consumeAccessProof`). **`processing -> paid` records verified transfer evidence** (unique cross-payout `transfer_evidence_ref` — duplicate bank transfer references across payouts are rejected with 409 `DUPLICATE_BANK_TRANSFER_REFERENCE` — plus a non-reusable transfer receipt artifact $\le 10\text{ MB}$ verified by magic-byte sniffing for PNG/JPEG/WebP/PDF and SHA-256 digest `EVIDENCE_REUSE_FORBIDDEN`, operator, time) and needs no second person; it writes no seller ledger row (already debited at request; bank transfer fees are absorbed by the platform). `rejected` inserts an opposing `payout_reversal` computed from the exact ledger entry of that `payout_id`; it is allowed from `requested`, and from `processing` only with evidence that no transfer was sent. An unknown transfer outcome **stays `processing`** for reconciliation against bank evidence: no presumed rejection, no automatic retransfer and no reversal until finance records the confirmed outcome (the same rule as affiliate payouts, ADR-0009 section 7).
   - **Bank-destination change controls (anti-fraud, not tax or KYC; decided mandatory by owner decision D5 / D-19):** these controls apply to all seller payouts from Bukalaris. Creating or changing a destination and requesting a payout each need a step-up, i.e. a fresh proof (MFA, or when MFA is not enrolled a one-time proof to the verified contact; freshness window in IAM-9/IAM-13). The previous verified contact is notified of every change. A new or changed destination is `held` for **72 hours** (`hold_until`, default 72 hours, Owner-configurable; the same 72-hour hold applies after credential recovery or MFA reset); payout requests use only an `active` destination whose hold has elapsed, and the account-holder name is checked against the store owner's verified name (`holder_name_check` shown to the four-eyes approver).
   - When a `paid` non-COD order is `cancelled` or `returned`, the same transaction opens one `refund` row for the **full** payment (partial refunds are unsupported, decision 4; composition in BILL-5), partial unique index on open refund per payment. The refund moves `pending -> processing` ("approve for transfer", four-eyes `approval_request`, action `refund.approve_transfer`, capability `refunds.process`) `-> completed | failed`; completing records transfer evidence only, and an unknown transfer outcome stays `processing`.

6. **AutoLaris payment adapter contract and verify-by-pull Advice reconciliation (T-49).**
   - **Why `/submit` instead of `/create_payment` (Tokophi production lesson):** Checkout computes the buyer-facing admin fee from the platform schedule (`payment_fees`, where each fee declares its explicit `base`: `QRIS` = **0.7% of goods omset** `subtotal - goods_discount`, never payable total; `VABCA` = **Rp6.500 flat**, other VA banks `VABNI`/`VABRI`/`VAMANDIRI`/`VAPERMATA` = **Rp3.000 flat**; e-wallet = **3.0% of payable total**) and sends `grand_total = String(buyer_payable_idr)` (`order_payable + admin_fee`) to `POST /api/h2h/submit`. On `/submit`, `total === grand_total` and AutoLaris nets its own `biaya_admin` against platform settlement rather than adding it on top (whereas `/create_payment` adds the provider fee on top of `amount`, causing double-charging and QRIS fee-on-shipping drift). Never call `/create_payment` for the same checkout.
   - **Required 24-key `/submit` payload for `courir_id: 1` (digital/payment-only profile):** The non-physical `courir_id: 1` profile (note vendor spelling `courir_id`, **not** `courier_id`) is an account agreement that AutoLaris must approve for Bukalaris before live use. Even for `courir_id: 1`, the `/submit` validator requires dummy logistics fields or it returns `rc=01 "Invalid parameter"`: `origin: 3517100`, `destination: 3518010`, `weight: "1"`, `length: "1"`, `width: "1"`, `height: "1"`, `cod_value: "0"`, static shipper fields, customer receiver fields (`receiver_address: "Pesanan digital"`), and one summary `order_details` row (`qty: "1"`, `unit_price: String(buyer_payable_idr)`). Validate that `rc === "00"` and that **exactly one** instruction field (`payment_info.va` / `virtual_account`, `qr`, `payment_code`, `url`) is non-empty and $\le 2048$ characters (99ribu guard).
   - **Two-phase attempt coordination & no-IO-under-lock:** Persist the stable local `reff_id` and `payment_attempt` (`request_state = 'in_progress'`, monotonic `payment_attempt_event` `revision + 1`) in a short DB transaction under an order-scoped advisory lock, **commit the transaction before calling AutoLaris over the network**, and then resolve the attempt (`accepted` with `transaction_id` and instructions, or `failed` on definite rejection; a timeout leaves the attempt `unknown`/pending so a retry or concurrent caller with the same `request_key` polls `awaitStoredResult` instead of creating a second bill).
   - **Container-independent WIB expiry arithmetic (`expiryStampWib`):** AutoLaris parses `expired` (`YYYYMMDDHHMMSS`) in **WIB (Asia/Jakarta, UTC+7)**. In UTC containers, local-time getters cause `rc=08 "Invalid expired date"` or silently shorten 24h bills to 17h. Shift UTC epoch by $+7\text{ hours}$ (`new Date(Date.now() + msFromNow + 7 * 3600 * 1000)`) and format using `getUTC*` getters; confirm `/submit` expiry support on the Bukalaris account and preserve the returned `payment_info.expired`.
   - **Authoritative channel discovery (`GET /api/h2h/list_payment`):** Parse active channels with a pure `parseListPayment` helper. **Absence from `data` is the sole disabled signal** (there is no `active: false` flag on provider rows). Never probe channel availability by calling `create_payment` (Tokophi incident: startup probes created 16 real unpaid bills per run). Preserve raw EMVCo QRIS strings byte-for-byte (never rewrite the merchant name, which invalidates the CRC).
   - **Bound per-payment callback HMAC (`?cb=<reff>.<hmac128>`), trigger-only handling & monotonicity guards:** Because AutoLaris echoes `callback_url` verbatim without custom headers, mint `?cb=<reff_id>.<hmac>` where `hmac = HMAC-SHA256(AUTOLARIS_CALLBACK_SECRET, "pay-callback:v1:" + reff_id).slice(0, 32)` (128-bit hex; shared secret never in URL). In `POST /api/payments/autolaris/callback` (`HTTP-17`): (1) verify HMAC in constant time (`timingSafeEqual`), (2) **bind** the signed `reff_id` to the resolved `payment_attempt.reff_id` (`boundRef === attempt.reff_id`, rejecting a valid callback URL replayed with another order's body with 403), (3) reject missing identity (`trx_id` and `reff_id` both empty) or $>1$ matching rows with 400, (4) verify safe-integer exact amount equality of the callback body against `attempt.total_idr` (on mismatch, log PII-redacted body shape `payCallbackBodyShape` — sorted keys, types, lengths — and reject), (5) **treat the callback as a trigger only (verify-by-pull, owner decision D-21):** record the raw body with `body_sha256` (the raw body is redacted after 30 days, `AUTOLARIS_CALLBACK_RETENTION_DAYS = 30`), enqueue one idempotent verification job for that attempt (below), and change no payment, order or ledger state in the request itself; the status words or amount in the callback body are never settlement evidence, so a callback never moves money by itself, and (6) keep **terminal-order quarantine**: a late `EXPIRED` or `PAID` callback for a `cancelled`/`returned` (terminal) order is recorded and quarantined, never applied; if the verification below returns `paid` for such an order, mark the attempt `paid` for audit only, do NOT mark the order paid or credit seller/affiliate ledgers, and open a buyer `refund` obligation. **Forward-only monotonicity** also stays (`WHERE status <> 'paid'`): neither a callback nor a verification result regresses a `paid` attempt.
   - **Verification by pull (the provider inquiry) & dead `/inquiry_payment` warning:** `POST /api/h2h/inquiry_payment` returns 404 on live accounts and must never be called. The provider inquiry is `POST /api/h2h/advice` with `{ transaction_id }` (provider ID, never local `reff_id`). It is called by the verification job a callback enqueues and by the bounded polling jobs (`ADVICE_MAX_POLLS = 24`, interval 300s, plus a final poll at expiry), through the same `classifyAdvice` rule: a payment is **`paid` only when the inquiry returns `rc` `00` AND status `PAID` AND the provider amount equals the attempt's `total_idr`**; `rc` `02` is `pending` (Tokophi observed `rc: "02", ket: "FAILED"` on live unpaid bills); **anything else is `unknown` and never settled**, including generic words (`SUCCESS`, `BERHASIL`, `SUKSES`, `DELIVERED`, `TERKIRIM`, `LUNAS`, `SETTLED`) and a `PAID` with a different amount or a non-`00` `rc`.
   - **Live gates stay until the vendor codes are observed in production:** the `rc` `00` / `PAID` / amount rule above is the owner-decided classification, but the vendor status codes are UNVERIFIED on a Bukalaris account. Until they are observed in production and the owner enables them in writing, `ADVICE_SETTLED = []` (observe-only): the verification and polling jobs record their classified result and settle nothing, and live settlement of a prepaid payment remains the existing confirmed operator action (no existing gate is removed). After that enablement a `paid` classification from the pulled inquiry becomes the only automated path to `paid`; a callback still never is.
   - Production requests require AutoLaris to whitelist the platform's egress IPs (maximum of five). All sandbox/real calls remain behind explicit owner approval.

7. **Mengantar booking reconciliation (T-37 amends the T-39 booking path).**
   - Booking is live-priced on the server from the order's own route and total parcel weight (sum of quantity x variant weight, kg), using the same derivation as the quote. Dimensions are not part of the documented Mengantar estimate or create-order fields (weight in kg only; when `customProducts` is sent, `weight` must equal the sum of `qty x weight`); whether volumetric weight is applied by couriers on Mengantar's side is UNVERIFIED, so a quote/booking price difference must be treated as a reconciliation signal. A browser-supplied cost is only compared to detect that the confirmation went stale; it is never the authoritative amount.
   - For every shipment (`partner_managed` and `platform_master` alike, D-15), booking has three steps and **never calls the provider while a transaction or lock is open**: (1) outside any lock, a read-only live re-quote of the order's route and weight at the booking COD amount; (2) one transaction takes the locks in the decision-4 order (order row, then the per-store payout lock), recomputes `shipping_delta` from the fresh quote, inserts the append-once `shipment_cost` record and the `shipping_debit` ledger entry, and refuses the booking **before** any provider write when the debit cannot be covered; commit; (3) after commit, the worker calls Create Order and records the result (provider identifiers, `shipment` state) in a second short transaction. A provider price that later differs from the reserved cost is a reconciliation signal, not a ledger mutation. This seller-ledger check is separate from T-39's `awaiting_balance`, which concerns the platform's own Mengantar balance. The interim seller-account path with no platform reservation or margin is superseded (D-15).
   - **Coverage rule:** the debit is covered when `available_balance_idr >= debit`, or, for a prepaid order (payment `paid`, its own `sale` `pending`), when `available_balance_idr + sale_amount >= debit`. In the second case the debit is inserted with `status = 'pending'` and is released with its sale at delivery, so it nets against the order's own pending sale in the held pool and never makes `available` negative; a new seller can therefore ship a free-shipping order (PR-26). If a prepaid order is `returned` or `cancelled` in transit, its sale is reversed against `pending` and the debit stays `pending` (it never moves to `available`) until the provider confirms the void (`shipping_debit_reversal`) or finance resolves it (OPEN-F6). A COD order has no sale at booking: it needs `available_balance_idr >= debit`; whether a zero-balance seller may instead book COD against a `pending` debit that is settled when the delivered sale is released is OPEN-F3 (recommended, not decided: allow it).
   - A prepaid order cannot reserve or book shipping before a successful payment exists. COD remains bookable while its payment is pending, because collection happens at the door, but only after T-35 confirmation.
   - **COD cash-collected equals `payable` (field mapping and equality check):** Mengantar documents COD value = goods value + shipping fee + COD fee. For a COD booking Bukalaris sets the amount the courier collects to `order_financials.payable` (which already contains the buyer shipping fee with the platform margin, any buyer COD fee and its VAT), reads the booking-basis provider `codFee` from the same re-quote taken at that COD amount (the quote-time basis, `COD_AMOUNT` = subtotal, is not used for booking), and sends the fields so that `goods_value_sent + shipping_fee_sent + cod_fee_sent = payable`, with `shipping_fee_sent` the provider's courier price and `cod_fee_sent` the booking-basis `codFee`. The provider's field names and which of them it validates are UNVERIFIED until an observed booking. The booking service checks the equality **before** the provider call (a mismatch refuses with `cod_amount_mismatch`), and a response that echoes a different COD amount is recorded as a reconciliation break. The booking-basis `codFee` is part of the booked cost used in `shipping_delta`.
   - A transport failure or timeout is an **unknown outcome**, not a provider rejection: retain the local booking intent, any returned provider identifiers and any reserved debit, and block blind rebooking. Mengantar Create Order documents **no client-supplied reference or idempotency key**: it returns `ORDER_ID` and `cnote_no` (and Mongo `_id`), so the outcome cannot be found by our own reference. Reconcile via paged `GET /api/public/{API_KEY}/order` under the **same account**, filtered by `dateRange` and `courier`, matching the submitted receiver and parcel details. A missing or ambiguous match stays in manual recovery; it never proves that creation failed. A definite provider rejection appends an opposing `shipping_debit_reversal` only if a platform debit was reserved.
   - **Cancellation contract** ([Mengantar Public API, "Delete Orders"](https://sandbox.mengantar.com/docs/), reviewed 2026-10-27): send `DELETE /api/public/{API_KEY}/order` with `courier` and exactly one of `ids` (Mongo `_id` values) **XOR** `orderIds` (`ORDER_ID` values), **not the AWB/resi**. A missing required order identity must be recovered through `GET /order` or manual recovery, not treated as a missing-AWB prohibition. Eligibility is `active`, `error`, `PENDING PICKUP`, `PICKUP FAILED` or `PICKUP_FAILED_API` (the docs also allow no status yet); orders past pickup are silently skipped. Anteraja requires at least five minutes after creation. HTTP 200 / `success` / `deletedCount` alone is not per-order success: confirm the order's Mongo `_id` is in `deletedOrderIds` (the response also lists `deletedOrderIdsHumanReadable`), retaining its provider identity and resi for audit. No generic queued-cancellation behaviour is assumed.
   - A confirmed deletion establishes cancellation, **not a wallet refund**. Keep any `shipping_debit` until the provider explicitly confirms that booking cost was voided/refunded; only then append its opposing `shipping_debit_reversal`. The invoice vocabulary contains `typeRefund`, but the cancellation documentation does not promise a wallet-cost refund or its timing.
   - Rebooking is not supported in M3: `shipment` is one row per order (`shipment_order_uq`), and rebooking needs an immutable booking-attempt record first. A cancelled or failed booking blocks a second booking until that record exists.

8. **Order-status and payment-callback invariants.**
   - Order status has one writer. It holds an explicit table of legal moves, same-status is a no-op, `cancelled` and `returned` are terminal, `returned` is reachable only from in-transit states, and `completed -> cancelled`, `completed -> returned` and `completed -> shipped` are illegal (a delivered parcel has already released or queued money; post-delivery claims are four-eyes adjustments, decision 4).
   - Provider-driven transitions may jump forward in one step, because tracking polls can miss or reorder intermediate events. A transition the writer rejects is recorded (shipment `last_error` and log) and never dropped silently; the job reports the outcome the writer returned, not the attempt.
   - `sale` credit is never booked for a `cancelled` or `returned` order. A payment that settles after the order is terminal is quarantined under the Q-10 policy (no silent fulfillment, no credit) and opens a buyer refund obligation.
   - A `paid` payment is never regressed by a later `expired` or `failed` event; money received is not un-collected by a webhook. Every callback matches amount and reference against the local payment record and applies idempotently; under decision 6 a callback only enqueues the verification and never settles by itself.
   - A terminal order cannot receive a new payment attempt, and a second attempt is refused while one is still pending.
   - AutoLaris callback authentication fails closed under decision 6: use the verified per-payment signed URL reference, never assume unsupported custom headers or accept unauthenticated production callbacks. A callback is only a trigger for the verify-by-pull inquiry and never moves money by itself; amount/reference checks and settlement gates remain mandatory.
   - **Mengantar webhook contract** ([Public API, "Webhook"](https://sandbox.mengantar.com/docs/), documented 2026-10-27, not yet observed by Bukalaris): `POST` JSON contains only `cnote_no`, `order_id`, `courier` and `status_category`; no payment amount or COD-remittance proof is present. Headers are `x-timestamp` (epoch milliseconds) and `x-signature` (hex HMAC-SHA256 of `{x-timestamp}.{raw body}`, keyed by that account's Webhook Secret). Capture exact raw bytes before parsing, verify in constant time and reject timestamps older than five minutes; unset/invalid secrets fail closed.
   - Acknowledge an authenticated, durably accepted event with **any 2xx within five seconds**, then process it idempotently. Mengantar retries a failure up to three times (~1s / 5s / 15s), best effort, and pauses delivery after 20 consecutive failures within 24 hours. Duplicates and out-of-order delivery are normal: use `x-timestamp` for newer-event comparison and the single status writer for legal transitions, never arrival order. Missed/paused deliveries are recovered through account-scoped `GET /order`; resuming delivery does not replay the gap.
   - Mengantar has **no fixed webhook source IP**, so an inbound IP allow-list is not an authentication option (this does not remove the separate Partner API production egress-IP requirement in ADR-0007). Sandbox adds `x-environment: sandbox`; production does not. Use separate URLs/environments and refuse sandbox events as production mutations; that header is not part of the HMAC. Production webhooks are enabled **per account**. Mengantar webhooks are received per Bukalaris-managed account; there is no seller-controlled URL or secret (D-15).
   - Documented `status_category` values include `PENDING PICKUP`, `PICKED UP`, `ON DELIVERY`, `DELIVERED`, `UNDELIVERED`, `PICKUP FAILED`, `RTS`, both `CANCELED` / `CANCELLED`, `ACTIVE/WAITING NEXT PROCESS` and `ERROR`. The vocabulary is **open-ended**, so retain a default branch. Proposed terminal/problem mapping: `DELIVERED -> completed`, `RTS -> returned`, `CANCELED | CANCELLED -> cancelled`; `UNDELIVERED | PICKUP FAILED` records a shipment **problem** with **no money effect**, not a new terminal order state. Unknown categories log only and do not mutate order/payment/ledger state. All mapped events still pass the status writer and custody rules; observed sandbox payloads are required before live money effects.

9. **Ledger reads and verification.**
   - Nothing that sums a balance may join `order`: `payout` entries have `order_id` NULL by design, so an inner join drops every withdrawal. Orphan checks count `order_id IS NULL` separately.
   - A ledger verification command, `pnpm verify:ledger` (to be built by T-37; contract, not existing code), run by the T-37 tests and available to operators, recomputes per store from `ledger_entry` alone and reports: entries by kind and status, entries whose `order_id` references no order, balances that disagree with the sum of their entries, and any violation of the ledger consistency invariants of decision 3 (one `sale` per order, at most one `return_reversal`, no negative `available`, payout entries paired with a `payout` row, `released_at` and `release_basis` on every released entry). It must not write.

10. **Provider evidence boundary and facts to verify before live.**
   - **Documented by Mengantar 2026-10-27, not yet observed by Bukalaris** ([official Public API](https://sandbox.mengantar.com/docs/); sections "Create Order", "Delete Orders", "Webhook", "Check Shipping Fee", "Pickup Time" and "Simulate Order Status"):
     - The status vocabulary and webhook HMAC/raw-body contract, best-effort delivery, lack of fixed source IP, and per-account production enablement in decision 8; this is documentary evidence, not a Bukalaris runtime result.
     - Cancellation identifiers, eligibility, per-order deletion result and Anteraja five-minute delay in decision 7. Create Order accepts no local-reference/idempotency field: preserve returned `ORDER_ID`, Mongo `_id` and `cnote_no`; do not import Etalashop's unrelated `maxLength 20` reference constraint or claim lookup by a client reference.
     - Pickup scheduling must be at least **90 minutes** ahead. The documented per-account sequential-batch/concurrency restriction is for **JT Premium and SiCepat**, with a 409 conflict shown for JT Premium; do not extend that rule to every courier or discontinued Ninja.
     - **Ninja is discontinued from 2026-09-01** for quotes, new shipments and paying unpaid shipments; historical order/batch reads remain available.
     - Create Order defines **COD value = goods value + shipping fee + COD fee**. This does not establish a fixed fee rate or remittance date.
     - **SPX:** maximum 50 kg, COD Rp5,000,000, non-COD goods value Rp10,000,000. **Pos COD:** at least 82% delivered rate across all couriers in the rolling 21–60 day window, with the documented grace/evaluation policy; account eligibility/`unsupported_cod` must still gate checkout rather than a guessed local exemption.
   - **Still UNVERIFIED before live use:**
     - COD remittance schedule, net deductions and settlement proof. Order `isReconciliated`, invoice `typeReconciliation`, and order filters `DELIVERED_PENDING` / `DELIVERED_COMPLETED` exist, but their settlement semantics are undocumented and the sandbox never triggers reconciliation: none authorizes a ledger release until observed and accepted by the owner (OPEN-F8); until then release is the Owner console reconciliation batch of decision 4.
     - COD fee rate, minimum and VAT treatment per courier/account; read dynamic `codFee` from `/order/estimate`, never hard-code an assumed percentage.
     - Whether cancellation returns the wallet booking cost, how to identify that refund and when it arrives; `typeRefund` alone is not a refund guarantee.
     - General Public API rate limits per key/endpoint; the separate Partner API budget in ADR-0007 is not a Public API limit.
   - **Observation path, still owner-gated:** sandbox-only `POST /order/simulation` can force `ON DELIVERY`, `DELIVERED` or `RTS`, then read `GET /order` / history and receive the configured webhook. It is the documented way to observe those contracts because sandbox orders do not advance by themselves. It returns 404 in production and has **no balance, COD payout, reconciliation, refund or fee effects**, so it cannot prove settlement. No simulation or real call was made for this ADR.
   - **Envelope invariant:** an HTTP 200 whose body reports failure is a provider error, not "no data"; validate the documented response body and per-order result before applying any state change.
   - **AutoLaris provenance:** the AutoLaris H2H integration notes used for this ADR (an internal agent-tooling skill, `autolaris-h2h`, not a repository path; its rules 2 and 4–8 and its payment-gateway guide are restated in decisions 6 and 8 and in BILL-2/BILL-4 so readers do not need it); the public [H2H collection](https://documenter.getpostman.com/view/25938923/2sB2iwFuwz); Tokophi `packages/lib/src/autolaris.ts` (`expiryStamp`, `alCreateOrder`, Advice observation comments), `packages/lib/src/pay-callback.ts`, and `apps/admin/app/api/public/pay/callback/route.ts`. These support decision 6's distinctions between public documentation, Tokophi observations and **UNVERIFIED Bukalaris account behaviour**; they are not Bukalaris implementation evidence.

## Alternatives considered

- *Store a mutable `store.balance_idr` column* — rejected: drifts from order history under retries or partial failures and cannot be audited.
- *Release seller balance when order status becomes `shipped`* — rejected: exposes the platform to unrecoverable loss on COD refusals and prepaid returns.
   - *Let each seller bring their own external Mengantar API key (`own_key`) or split into two money systems* — rejected by the owner (2026-10-27): Bukalaris instead provisions a Partner-managed Mengantar account per store under ADR-0007 so 100% of shipping management, discounts, margin, and cashback stay in Bukalaris's hands under one unified ledger.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** closes the proposed ledger design of Q-17 and T-37 with database-enforced idempotency; aligns Dashboard revenue, Order list, and Seller Balance by construction; no seller money is ever `available` for an undelivered or unreconciled parcel, so a negative balance cannot arise; puts every COD and prepaid order under one Bukalaris-managed ledger (D-15, D-16).
- **Negative:** adds `order_financials`, `ledger_entry`, `payout`, `refund`, `reconciliation_batch` and `reconciliation_batch_item` tables, the `ledger.release` job, an Owner console reconciliation batch workflow (manual until the provider signal is verified) and the `returned` order transition before live COD dispatches can be enabled (`ALLOW_COD_BOOKING=1`). Seller COD proceeds wait for reconciliation, which is slower than a fixed delay; sellers see them as `pending`. COD stays gated on the unverified free-return terms. Partner account provisioning adds the credential boundaries in ADR-0007. Documented provider behaviour still requires owner-approved observation before live use.
- **Neutral:** platform subscription billing (`PR-18` / BILL-7 / T-50) is no longer deferred; it remains a separate platform invoice/payment domain, never buyer orders or seller proceeds.
