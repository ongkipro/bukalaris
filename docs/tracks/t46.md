## Decisions and defaults taken (revisable, none is an owner decision of substance)

1. **Flash sale = price override, applied first** (PR-26), even when a voucher is older; the voucher then acts on the remaining net. Overlapping live flash sales on one variant: the oldest wins. A flash sale depleted by its cap stops applying (orders then pay the base price); a cart quantity above the remaining cap is refused (`flash_stock`), never silently split.
2. **Windows include both endpoints** (`starts_at <= now <= ends_at`, PR-26 text); the clock is the database's `clock_timestamp()` read after the shared promotion lock, never the caller's or the browser's. Seller-typed times are WIB (`jakartaInstant`).
3. **Usage is reserved at committed order creation, not at payment**, and is not returned by a cancellation or return (conservative anti-abuse default, as in T-49's one-attempt rule). The owner may decide to recycle.
4. **Percent values are integer basis points; money integer IDR.** The rounding rule is the per-store setting `promo_hub.percent_rounding` (`floor` default, `half_up`, `ceil`), applied once to the voucher's eligible base.
5. **BILL-4 shipping**: a seller-funded shipping discount (free-shipping voucher or the automatic threshold) covers at most the courier cost (`order.shipping_provider_cost`); the platform margin is always paid by the buyer, so `shipping_fee >= shipping_margin`. The automatic threshold compares the NET goods after discounts and does not stack on a shipping voucher. A promotion whose shipping subsidy exceeds the seller's sale amount is BLOCKED with a visible message (`promotion_negative_proceeds`) and consumes nothing. No platform fee, subsidy rate or margin-funded promotion exists.
6. **Redeem path abuse controls** (named defaults, D-22 item 3): 20 attempts per 10 minutes per trusted IP and 10 per 10 minutes per canonical phone per store (`VOUCHER_REDEEM_LIMITS`), counted by the shared limiter outside the order transaction so failures count. The phone part of the key is a keyed fingerprint (purpose `rate.voucher_phone`). Codes are 96-bit random, compared by digest with `timingSafeEqual` over equal-length values. Unknown, expired, not-yet-started, disabled and exhausted codes give ONE message and code (`voucher_unavailable`). Below-minimum and out-of-scope messages are distinct (they need a live code); with 96-bit codes this is no practical oracle, and UX-16 asks for the below-minimum feedback.
7. **Per-buyer cap key** = T-57 `phoneLookupKey` (every spelling of one number is one buyer), fingerprinted under every key of the ring so a key rotation does not reset caps. This is a phone-number cap, not an identity (no OTP exists), so it stops casual abuse only.
8. **Disable, never delete**: vouchers and flash sales are `disabled`; counters and redemption evidence are never deleted by a login role (guards + no grants); the owner-only demo reset is the single door.
9. **The order row carries both discounts** (`order.goods_discount`, `order.shipping_discount`) so the table's own CHECKs stay exact identities and the buyer, seller and mail views reconcile (`subtotal - goods discount + shipping = total`). The immutable snapshot's guard asserts they equal `order_financials`.

## DEFERRED (exact missing decision or dependency)

- **Booking after a seller-funded shipping discount** (T-48's `shipment_cost`): the current `reserveShippingCost` and `shipment_cost_identity_chk`/`one_side_chk` compute `debit - margin = booked - charged`; for an order with `shipping_discount = D` they would record margin 0 and a smaller debit. T-48 (agreed by message) copies `shipping_discount_idr` onto `shipment_cost` and books `debit = max(booked - (charged + D), 0) + D`, `margin = max(-(booked - (charged + D)), 0)`, replacing the one-side constraint. **Not testable in this tree**; after folding, the test to add is "an order with `shipping_discount = courier cost` books the seller debit D and keeps the platform margin". Everything on the checkout side (the snapshot, the order identity, the guard) is built and tested.
- **Custom hub slug** (`promo_hub.slug` in DATA-8): the hub is fixed at `/promo`; a different slug needs the owner's decision on reserved paths and redirects.
- **Recycling a voucher use after a cancellation or return** (decision 3): needs the owner's abuse-versus-fairness decision.
- **Sales-tax price modes with discounts at checkout** (T-37/T-65 not wired into checkout): the engine supports them and the allocation fixtures cover tax-included; `order_total_chk` assumes `tax_included` or no tax and must gain the added-tax term when T-65 wires `tax_added`.
- **Identity-bound voucher caps** (one person, not one phone number): needs T-58 buyer verification.
- **Platform-funded or margin-funded promotions** (T-48 shipping discounts, platform cashback): not an owner decision and not part of T-46.
