## Decisions and defaults taken (all revisable; none is a vendor or legal fact)

1. **Plan = a named row**, `subscription_plan` (`all_feature`, version 1): Rp175.000 DPP, trial 3, grace 3, PPN off, seeded by the migration; an Owner change appends a version effective at its creation instant (prospective). Price: four-eyes `billing.plan_price`; days: `billing.manage`; PPN: `tax.manage` (D-17).
2. **Trial/anchor**: the store-insert trigger writes `trial_ends_at = created_at + trial_days` and `billing_anchor_date` = its Jakarta date (write-once trigger + CHECK). Existing stores get a fresh 3-day trial at migration time (so nobody is locked out by the migration; the owner may decide otherwise).
3. **Access state is derived, never trusted from a cron**: `deriveBillingState` runs on every `sellerContext`; the cron only persists it (status, `past_due_at`, `grace_ends_at`) for the Owner console and dunning. Grace is counted from the instant coverage ended with the plan's `grace_days`.
4. **Read-only policy**: allowed while `past_due`/`suspended` = `catalog.read`, `orders.read`, `privacy.manage` (buyer-data export/erasure: a data-protection duty, never behind a debt), `billing.read`, `billing.pay`. Everything else, including any capability added later, is refused with `billing_read_only`. Checkout/storefront are untouched; only `store.status = 'suspended'` (with `suspended_at`) refuses checkout.
5. **`store.suspended_at`**: the operator's existing `status` switch now also owns the timestamp (trigger keeps the two in lock-step, CHECK says they cannot disagree). Billing, payment and comp have no write path to either.
6. **Invoice window**: the invoice for period `k` may be created from `INVOICE_LEAD_MS` (named, 3 days = the trial length) before its start, so period 0 exists from the day a store is created and renewal can be paid before access lapses. A seller may also Pay period 0 earlier than the window (BILL-7). Never a period whose end has passed (no back-billing, OPEN-B1, D-20): elapsed unpaid invoices are closed `void` / `lapsed` by the cycle.
7. **One live/uncertain attempt per invoice** = partial unique index (`status in (pending, unknown) and request_state <> failed`). A timeout stays `unknown` and live; an accepted instruction is replaced only after `expires_at + 5 min` (named margin) through the definer `subscription_expire_attempt` (the app role holds no UPDATE). A payment arriving on an expired attempt still settles (money received) once; a second payment for an already-covered or void invoice extends nothing and leaves a `payment_on_covered_invoice` / `payment_on_void_invoice` event for a refund decision (T-37).
8. **Fee**: `admin_fee_idr` of a subscription attempt is 0 (the bearer is OPEN in DATA-10); settlement compares the provider amount with the attempt's `total_idr`, so either answer works later.
9. **Comp / mark-paid / price** are four-eyes through the T-60 matrix (`billing.comp`, `billing.mark_paid`, `billing.plan_price`): request, second operator decides in Persetujuan, the requester executes with the approval id; the approval is bound to the exact payload and consumed in the mutation's transaction. Mark-paid is bookkeeping only (no coverage, no provider evidence); comp is a Rp0 `kind=comp` paid invoice with explicit coverage, voids an unpaid invoice of that period first, refuses a paid period.
10. **Dunning** = schedule + message intents only: `subscription_notice` (unique per store, kind, reference) written in the same transaction as the `mail_enqueue` call, owners only, OFF by default (`billing_control.dunning_enabled`), the wording never says "sent" (UX-18). Timings are named settings (1-day leads). Real delivery is DEFERRED (below).
11. **PPN**: no rate anywhere in `packages/db/src/billing` (repo-scan test). The snapshot is computed by the T-65 engine from the effective confirmed flat `vat_output`/`subscription` policy and re-computed in SQL by `subscription_invoice_guard`; an invoice insert and a plan with `ppn_enabled = true` are both refused without an effective policy (service **and** database). A non-flat (bracketed) policy is refused (`policy_invalid`): a subscription has one line.
12. **Callback route**: the same URL serves order and invoice references; the signed reference selects the table (order attempt looked up first); a subscription callback stores only digest and shape (no body).
13. **Demo seed**: platform evidence is append-only, so `reset_demo_billing(bigint[])` (owner connection only, `demo-%` stores only) clears the demo stores' billing rows.
