## What changed

New (`packages/db/src/shipping/`): `schema-accounts.ts` (`store_shipping_account`, `shipping_control`, `cashback_rule`, `shipping_partner_budget`, `shipping_dispatch_lease`), `credentials.ts` (AES-256-GCM key ring `KEYRING_SHIPPING_CREDENTIALS`, AAD = store + account + kind, `key_id` in the envelope, purpose-separation check against the integration/tax/buyer keys; the unseal primitive is not exported by `index.ts`), `accounts.ts` (account views with an allowlist, routing, provisioning, recovery, JOB-08 list reconciliation, JOB-23 re-seal, automatic sweep), `jobs.ts` (worker-only registrations, like `billing/jobs.ts`), `policy.ts` (four-eyes settings, margin, discount, cashback drafts, courier policy), `dispatch.ts` (durable per-account dispatch lease instead of the old advisory lock), `reconciliation.ts` (per-account summary), `copy.ts` (`whiteLabel`), `mengantar/gate.ts` (entry gate), `mengantar/partner.ts` (Partner client + fake transport).

- **Provisioning**: every store gets a `platform_master` (active) and a `partner_managed` (pending) binding from a store-insert trigger (and a backfill); `external_user_id = 'store:<id>'` is a CHECK. The worker sweep (`shipping.account.sweep`, every minute, 10 stores per run) registers through the fake Partner client (`POST /api/partner/v1/sellers`) with a per-store routing e-mail (`store-<id>@<MENGANTAR_ROUTING_EMAIL_DOMAIN>`, fake default `.invalid`); `409 EMAIL_ALREADY_REGISTERED` retries with a fresh e-mail (3 attempts), then the store stays on the master account and the retry backs off 15 minutes. One claim lease plus the unique `(store_id, kind)` index give one account under concurrency. No `own_key`: the kind CHECK has two values and no write path accepts a key.
- **Credentials**: sealed with the key ring; the app role has a column grant without `sealed_key`, `key_id`, `registered_email`, `mengantar_seller_id`; every DTO is an allowlist (`id, storeId, kind, status, lastCheckedAt, lastResult, revision`); results are codes only; transport errors are reduced to a code.
- **Snapshot**: `order`, `order_financials` and `shipment_cost` carry `(shipping_account_id, shipping_account_kind)` under a compound FK `(store_id, id, kind)`; a definer trigger derives it inside the writer transaction, requires an active account, forbids any UPDATE, and refuses a financial/cost row that differs from the order's (one source of truth: the order). `shipment_cost.shipping_discount_idr` is copied from the financials.
- **Booking / tracking** use the snapshotted account only. A needs-key or disabled account stops the shipment visibly (`awaiting_account`, retryable, nothing reserved, nothing sent, no failover to the master). A partner snapshot is never dispatched through the master credential and live partner dispatch stays refused (OPEN-F9). Provider writes are serialised per account by a committed lease (`409` retry path unchanged).
- **BILL-4 funding** (agreed with T-46): `delta = booked − (charged + discount)`, `debit = max(delta,0) + discount`, `margin = max(−delta,0)`; `shipment_cost_one_side_chk` replaced by `shipment_cost_discount_chk`.
- **Reconciliation**: `reconciliation_batch.shipping_account_id`; `prepareReconciliationBatch` refuses a mixed batch (`account_mismatch`); a trigger (ordered after the T-37 guards) requires an account on a new batch, forbids changing it, and requires an item's order to belong to the batch account; the batch hash and the approval payload include the account; applying needs the four-eyes approval bound to it. SA-53 lists batches with the account and a per-account summary. The provider signal path (OPEN-F8) is untouched and off.
- **Settings** (all named, conservative, D-22): `shipping_control` (prefer partner **off** until OPEN-F9, fixed/percent shipping discount 0, cashback off), changed only through four-eyes `shipping.margin` (request, second operator, requester applies; the database trigger requires the executed approval of exactly these values). The margin rule moved to the same flow. A discount is funded from the platform margin only (`min(margin, ...)`). `cashback_rule` rows can be drafted in the console but a CHECK forces `status = 'disabled'` and `cashback_enabled` cannot be true: no effective cashback rule, snapshots stay 0. `courier_service_policy` is edited in SA-07 (SPX seed untouched; a case-insensitive unique index prevents a COD-allowed spelling shadowing a refusal).
- **Console / seller**: SA-07 `/admin/shipping` extended (gate state, margin, discount and routing, cashback drafts, courier policy, account health with key recovery); SA-53 gains the per-account section; SC-22 `/seller/shipping` shows which account is used (new read capability `shipping.read`, owner and staff, in `BILLING_READ_ONLY_ALLOWED`). Seller/buyer copy now says "Bukalaris Kirim" (fulfilment messages, status labels, timeline, seller order page; provider error text scrubbed at display by `whiteLabel`).

## Entry gate (RUNBOOK terms for the conductor)

Before the first provider call of any kind (sandbox and read-only included) the owner: (1) asks Mengantar for a replacement Partner Secret; (2) stores it only in the secret store; (3) confirms in writing that the old secret is revoked; (4) records the rotation in RUNBOOK without the value; (5) only then sets `MENGANTAR_PARTNER_SECRET_ROTATED_AT=<UTC ISO timestamp, in the past>` and `MENGANTAR_PARTNER_OLD_SECRET_REVOKED=1` (both required; any other value keeps the gate shut). The gate is checked in `MengantarClient.#request` (every read and write, 3PL included) and in the live path of `PartnerClient` before the rate-limit budget is spent; the fake transport is unaffected. `/admin/shipping` shows the state as "Terkunci" without any value. Also set `KEYRING_SHIPPING_CREDENTIALS` (`<key_id>:<base64 32 bytes>[,old...]`, first is current; keep an old key until the re-seal job leaves no envelope under it). A production worker without a live Partner adapter refuses to provision (it never mints fake accounts); the live transport itself is not built.
