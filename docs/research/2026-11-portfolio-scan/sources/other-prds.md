# TokoPhi/CMSPRO/PunyaSitus/GeraiCUAN family — findings for Bukalaris

## Source summary
- Maturity: all are spec or research documents. None is runtime evidence, and the browser, provider and security behaviour in them was not tested.
- Date range: 2026-09-01 to 2026-10-28. The PunyaSitus folder is a non-authoritative snapshot (`punyasitus/README.md:1`). The tokoku PRD is a generic stub.
- Bukalaris already covers most of this (see "Partly covered" and the targets below). It is stricter than the source on net-proceeds money, host routing and RLS.
- I read the TokoPhi, GeraiCUAN, Formalin and email/WhatsApp notes directly. The CMSPRO and punyasitus/landing-v2 packs were scanned by subagents, and I did not re-verify their line numbers or the Bukalaris grep matches. Some TokoPhi paths below are code paths in a repo I did not open.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Crediting the seller the gross order amount including shipping. The earlier TokoPhi ADR 0014 did this, and the note says to supersede it with tests (`N:33,135`).
- Letting the seller pick a different courier service later without a delta and a confirmation. The old behaviour moved no money for the difference (`N:18-19`).
- Rendering the raw QRIS payload as text (`N:22`).
- Treating a payment mismatch as automatic revocation. Quarantine it instead (`C/docs/spec/11-BILLING-PAYMENTS.md:85,87`).
- Using a bare `Math.round` or a combined `discount` column for money (`N:66`).
- Inferring an unpublished provider endpoint, such as using the order `/submit` for subscription charges (`R/formalin-my-t50-autolaris-billing-2026-09-01.md:20`).
- Writing market claims as fact when they are grade C or N, such as the OTP-versus-Google sign-in preference (`R/tokophi-account-market-indonesia-2026-10.md:28-31`).
- A cosmetic "invited" state or an empty promise of email delivery before the real mechanism exists (`R/geraicuan-tokophi-2026-09-14/ADMIN-SYSTEM-REVIEW.md:60,137`).
- Describing an amended PRD with both old and new designs left in the body, so sections 6, 7 and 12 contradicted section 17 (`L/PRD.md:419-480`).
- Redirecting unknown hosts or caching routing authority in KV (`P/PLAN.md:48,50`).
- A same-bucket backup mirror counted as a recovery copy (`C/docs/spec/15-DEVOPS-CICD-MIGRATIONS.md:74`).

## Not applicable to Bukalaris
- Agency accounts and multi-tenant access (CMSPRO ADR-0011). Bukalaris has no agency tier.
- Website-builder items: section registry, template editor, tenant forms, image pipeline, WhatsApp mini-catalog. Bukalaris is a curated commerce store builder.
- Midtrans and the secondary-gateway SHA-512 formula. The pack marks them unverified, and Bukalaris is on AutoLaris.
- Caddy on-demand TLS and the Vultr/Coolify specifics. The pack itself marks the Caddy approach superseded, and Cloudflare-for-SaaS limits and prices need re-verification.
- PunyaSitus pricing, caps and trial numbers (IDR 98,500 per 30 days, 3-day trial and grace, 2 hostnames, 5 members). They are product choices, not invariants.
- The PunyaSitus "not a store" positioning. It is the opposite of Bukalaris's scope.
- The tokoku PRD. It is a generic stub with nothing specific to learn.
- The Astro single-runtime reversal (ADR-0002 to ADR-0007). Bukalaris is Next.js.
- GeraiCUAN shipment, RTS and contact-directory items, and the Tokophi 10-year/5-year retention figures. Retention is KUP-specific, so re-verify it for Bukalaris.
