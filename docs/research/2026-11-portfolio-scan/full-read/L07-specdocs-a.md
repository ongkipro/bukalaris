- Coolify-specific Laravel-encrypted env, `application_deployment_queues` shared across apps, Vultr/Hetzner hosting history, Traefik default-priority arithmetic: Bukalaris hosting is not decided at that level, and `DEPLOY-COOLIFY.md` already carries the generic rules.
