## Not applicable to Bukalaris

- ADR 0001, 0005, 0006 (mock-first, npm workspaces, shared nav source): Bukalaris is database-first, so the frozen-mock-date failure class cannot arise.
- ADR 0007 (PostgREST + JWT claim → GUC): Bukalaris uses separate DB pools and roles. Finding 1 is the surviving lesson.
- ADR 0008 (per-client storefront deploys, reversed to SSR multi-tenant by ADR 0009): Bukalaris was designed multi-tenant from the start.
- ADR 0003 "no Apps marketplace", ADR 0009 Puck: Bukalaris has its own curated-builder ADR-0003. The renderer rule is finding 8.
- ADR 0012 worker-versus-cron mapping, Meta OAuth and asset-selection flow: Bukalaris signals are a per-store pixel with a sealed token and an outbox job. Meta Catalog push is out of scope.
- KiriminAja or AutoLaris specifics (kelurahan ids, centroid coordinates, `force_insurance`, `credit/balance`, VA fee tables): Bukalaris uses Mengantar. Only the patterns in finding 3 transfer.
- The Cloudflare Email Obfuscation script (1,621 ms), the Astro `Stars.astro` DOM trimming, `srcset` byte tables and the checkout "(13 layanan)" label: specific to the Astro storefront.
- Subscription prices, fee tables and the six-province SPX COD list in the help centre: TokoΦ product facts, and they need re-verification if reused. COD, VA and QRIS figures also appear in two forms in the sources.
- `tokophi123` shared dev password and the weak 24-bit temp password: Bukalaris seed uses a documented local-only value and refuses to run in production.
- ADR 0016 and ADR 0018: both are Proposed in the source, not accepted decisions. Only the display and receivable rules in finding 23 are reusable.
- `apps/account` visual defects such as column voids, chip clipping and the bottom-bar overlap: they depend on the TokoΦ shell, not on a product rule.
